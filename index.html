Who I am

A Brief Biography

I am a first-year Software Engineering student at Miva Open University with a strong interest in building websites and applications that solve real problems. My journey into tech started in secondary school when I built my first web page with nothing but Notepad and curiosity.

Since then, I've been learning HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Python, and I enjoy turning ideas into clean, user-friendly interfaces. Outside the classroom I take part in coding challenges, help classmates debug their code, and contribute to my department's tech club.

My goal is to become a full-stack developer who builds accessible technology for education and small businesses.
